Kargi

Marketplace Delivery Platform

Kargi is a mobile application that connects customers with local retail stores in Batumi, Georgia.

Users can browse products, place orders, and receive delivery from nearby stores through a single platform.

The platform provides payment processing, order management, and customer support.

How it works

  • Customers select products from partner stores
  • Orders are placed and paid through the platform
  • Stores prepare the order
  • Delivery is performed by a third-party courier service

We collect only the information necessary to provide our services, including account details, order information, and contact data.

This data is used to process orders, improve service quality, and provide customer support.

We do not sell personal data to third parties. Information may be shared with partner stores and delivery providers strictly for order fulfillment.

Refund Policy

If there is an issue with an order (missing items, incorrect items, or quality concerns), users can contact support.

Each case is reviewed individually. Depending on the situation, a partial or full refund may be issued.

Refunds are processed back to the original payment method.

Account Deletion Guide

You can delete your account directly from the app by following these steps:

1. Open your account

Tap on the account icon on the main screen.

2. Choose account deletion

Select the "Delete account" option at the bottom of the list.

3. Confirm deletion

Confirm the account deletion in the dialog.

What will be deleted

All your personal data, saved payment methods, and draft orders will be permanently deleted. Order history will remain only in anonymized form.

This action cannot be undone.
